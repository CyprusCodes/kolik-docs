# Meeting rooms

Open [Meeting Rooms](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/meetings-room) from the sidebar. The page has **Rooms** and **Bookings** tabs.

## Find or create a room

Room cards show capacity, location, and equipment. The **calendar** icon shows availability; **book** opens the booking dialog for that room. Company and super administrators can add, edit, and delete rooms. In the room form, **name**, **capacity**, and **location** are required, and equipment is optional. Names must be at least two characters long; capacity must be between 1 and 1,000 people. Missing or invalid information triggers a field error.

## Make a booking

Create a booking under **Bookings** or start from a room card. Choose a **Room**, enter a **Title**, **Date**, **Start** and **End** times, and select participants. Saving adds the booking to the list. With appropriate permission, you can edit a row or delete it after confirmation.

Titles must be at least three characters long; the date cannot be in the past, and the end time must follow the start. The form warns if participants exceed room capacity. If the server reports a clash or another save error, read the message and change the time range or room. Deletion errors also show a notice; do not assume the operation succeeded until the list updates.
