# Employees

Open [Employees](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/employees) from the sidebar. Search under **All Employees** and switch between table, card, and organization-chart views. Click an employee to open their profile.

## Add an employee

Company administrators, super administrators, and HR managers can use **Add New Employee**. The adjacent arrow menu offers three options: **Send invitation**, **Add manually**, and **Bulk upload**. An invitation lets someone join by email; manual entry creates the personnel record directly; bulk upload is for many records. Complete required fields and check the result. Invitees appear under **Pending Employee Invitations**. **Not Joined Yet** is available only to company and super administrators.

## Employee profile

Open an employee row to access **Personal Information**, **Career**, **Leave**, **Bank Details**, **Salary**, **Documents**, and **Custom Fields**. **Timesheets** appears only when its app is enabled; **Salary** visibility also depends on the employee settings. If authorized, add or edit records from the relevant tabs. Bank accounts, contracts, and document records have their own edit/delete confirmations.

:::tip If you cannot find a record
Clear the search term first, then check **Pending Invitations** and **Not Joined Yet**. If a tab is absent, your account may not have the required administrative permission.
:::
