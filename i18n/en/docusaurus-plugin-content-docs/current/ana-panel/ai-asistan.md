# AI Assistant

Open [AI Assistant](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/ai) from the sidebar. A **Beta** label appears at the top and the chat area below. Write a message and click Send or press Enter; the answer appears in the conversation. The AI chat card on the dashboard also provides quick access.

Chat history is kept in the context of the selected organization and user. If you switch organizations, earlier conversations may not be visible. A loading indicator appears while the page loads. If sending fails, check that the message is not empty, verify the selected organization and session, and check your connection. Because this is a beta feature, verify answers about leave, payroll, and personal data against the relevant record pages.
