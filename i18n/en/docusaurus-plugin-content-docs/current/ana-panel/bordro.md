# Payroll

Authorized users can open [Payroll](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/payroll) from the sidebar. Access depends on payroll-management roles such as company administrator, super administrator, HR manager, or accounting manager. The page has **Payroll Groups** and **Payroll Runs** tabs.

## Payroll groups

Search and sort groups under **Payroll Groups**. In **New Group**, select a group name and organization bank account; the currency comes from that account. Open a group row to inspect current members and payment information in the details panel. If authorized, you can delete the group. To export a statement for another organization, select that organization under **Export**.

## Payroll run

**Payroll Runs** has **All**, **Pending**, **Processing**, **Completed**, and **Failed** status filters. Use **New Payroll Run** to choose a payroll group and create a run. Row actions let you process it, open its result file or bank authorization document, and—if authorized—mark it **Completed/Failed** or delete it.

:::tip If something fails
If groups or runs do not load, read the error notice and check your organization and role. A new run cannot be saved until the group and other required fields are filled. Before retrying a **Failed** run, inspect its details and generated documents.
:::

Do not confuse the main-menu **Payroll** page with work entries and payroll settings in the [Timesheets app](../uygulamalar/zaman-cizelgeleri).
