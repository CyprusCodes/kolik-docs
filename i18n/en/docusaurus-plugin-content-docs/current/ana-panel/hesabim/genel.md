# General profile information

[My Account → General](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/account?tab=general) contains three cards: **Basic Information**, **Personal Information**, and **Emergency Contacts**. The last two appear only if an employee record is linked to your account in the selected organization.

## Photo, name, and date of birth

**Basic Information** shows your profile photo, first and last name, date of birth, and email address. Change your name or date of birth and click **Save**; email is read-only in this form. Photo change/removal buttons appear for company administrators or users for whom the organization's **allow employees to edit their own photo** setting is enabled.

First and last names cannot be empty, and a future date of birth is invalid. Saving without changes may show a “no changes to save” notice. Failed photo uploads or removals show an error notification.

## Personal information

Under **Personal Information**, update marital status, number of children, education level, passport number, and identity number. The number of children cannot be negative. Click that card's **Save** button; it saves separately from Basic Information. This card may be absent if the employee record cannot be loaded.

## Emergency contacts

Under **Emergency Contacts**, click **Add Contact**. **Name** and **phone number** are required. Email and relationship are optional; an entered email must be valid. After saving, use **Edit** or **Delete** in the list. Deletion requires confirmation. If the list cannot be loaded, an error appears; check the selected employee and organization before saving.
