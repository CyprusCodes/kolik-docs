# Notification preferences

In [My Account → Notifications](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/account?tab=notifications), use the toggles to choose the notifications you want. Click **Save** below for changes to take effect; switching a toggle alone does not save it.

| Notification | Who can see it? |
| --- | --- |
| Replies to my leave request, support ticket, or meeting room request | General user roles. |
| My document is expiring | General user roles. |
| New leave request, employee document expiry, daily list of employees on leave | Company and HR administrators. |
| New support ticket | Company and IT administrators. |
| New meeting room request | Company administrators. |
| Timesheet entry awaiting approval | Company administrators. |

Visible toggles depend on your role; some do not appear for employee accounts. If preferences fail to load or save, the page shows a notice. Successful saving shows a confirmation. Some **document expiry** options may still have English labels in the frontend.
