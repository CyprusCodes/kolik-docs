# My performance and reviews

If the Performance app is enabled for your organization, open [My Performance](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/performance/my-performance) from the sidebar. For employees, this link may appear as **My Reviews**. A badge shows the number of pending reviews; if there is only one task, the link may open it directly.

## Pending review

Open forms assigned to you under **My Reviews**. Check the person being reviewed, period, your relationship (self, manager, peer, etc.), and status. Answer the questions and complete the form's save/submit steps. If a review link cannot be found or loaded, use **Try Again**. If it still fails, ask your manager to confirm the task is assigned to you.

## Results and goals

Depending on your permissions, select a period in **My Performance** to view results, KPI/OKR goals, and your own progress records. The current results page offers **Export PDF**. Basic employee accounts may open directly on the reviews list, so not everyone sees a results tab.

For administrator-side creation of periods, KPIs, and OKRs, see the [Performance app guide](../uygulamalar/performans).
