# Home

Open the [Dashboard](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard) from the sidebar. It shows remaining leave balance, team absences, quick links, an AI chat card, public holidays, and upcoming birthdays. Company administrators may also see an announcements card and management information such as trial-plan usage.

Use **Quick Links** to open frequently used pages. If the leave balance or team information looks wrong, check that you selected the correct organization. Open [Leave](./izinler) for detailed leave records or [Employees](./calisanlar) for employee information. The cards summarize information or navigate to detail pages; they do not create records themselves.
