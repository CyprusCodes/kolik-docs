# Company bank accounts

![Four sections of the bank account form](/img/en/admin/bank-account-sections.svg)

*Illustrative form diagram, not a screenshot.*

[Admin Panel → Company Bank Details](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/admin-panel?tab=company-banking) lists the bank, account details, account holder, number of contact people, currency, status, and creation date. Use **Refresh** to update the list, and pagination to browse it.

## Add or edit an account

1. Click **Add Bank Account**. To change an existing account, use the **edit** icon on its row.
2. In **Bank account details**, choose a bank. If it is not listed, use **add new bank**. Enter the branch code, account number, SWIFT code, and IBAN.
3. In **Account holder details**, enter the name, address, city, and country. In **Account settings**, choose a currency and whether the account is active.
4. Optionally add **bank contact people**. For each person, you can enter a first and last name, position, phone numbers, and multiple email addresses. One person can be marked as the primary contact.
5. Click **Create** or **Update**. In edit mode, the update button stays disabled until something changes.

:::warning About the delete button
Although a delete icon appears in the list, deletion is currently unavailable; clicking it does not remove the account. If an account needs to be removed, contact your administrator or support team.
:::
