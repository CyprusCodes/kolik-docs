# Manage company information

[Admin Panel → Company Information](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/admin-panel?tab=company-details) displays the company name, business type, website, and country. The **Edit** button is available to company administrators.

1. Click **Edit**.
2. Review and update **Company name**, **Website**, **Business type**, and **Country**. The website must be a valid address.
3. Click **Save**. The summary card reflects the saved information.

If the company belongs to a group, the **Company Group** diagram below shows the parent and associated companies and lets you switch between them. If authorized, **Add Company** opens a dialog for adding another company to the group. This area does not appear for standalone companies.

You cannot delete a company from this tab.
