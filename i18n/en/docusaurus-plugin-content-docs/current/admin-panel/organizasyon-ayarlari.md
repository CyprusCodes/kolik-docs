# Organization settings

![Three subtabs of organization settings](/img/en/admin/organization-settings-map.svg)

*Illustrative tab diagram, not a screenshot.*

[Admin Panel → Organization Settings](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/admin-panel?tab=organization-settings) has three subtabs.

## Document expiry notifications

In [Document Expiry](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/admin-panel?tab=organization-settings&subTab=document-expiry), select notification times for each document type: **one month before**, **two weeks before**, and **on the expiry date**. Choose whether notifications go to the **employee**, **managers**, and **HR**, then click **Save**. You cannot add or delete document types here.

## Employee settings

Under [Employees](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/admin-panel?tab=organization-settings&subTab=employees), configure **employee self-service access** and the associated permissions to **view salary information** and **upload a profile photo**. The salary-visibility toggle is unavailable when self-service is off. Click **Save Changes** after making your selections.

## Email configuration

In [Email Configuration](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/admin-panel?tab=organization-settings&subTab=email-configuration), Kolik's default email service is used when **Use your own email server** is off. When on, enter the SMTP server, port, username/password, sender address/name, reply-to address, and contact address. You can upload or remove the email logo. After clicking **Save Changes**, enter your own address under **Send test email** and check delivery.

:::caution SMTP credentials
Enter the password only through a secure administrator account; do not share it in screenshots or customer documentation. A saved password is not displayed in plain text in the form.
:::
