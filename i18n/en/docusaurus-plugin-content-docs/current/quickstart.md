# Getting started with Kolik

After your initial setup, employees can submit leave requests and managers can track them.

## First steps

1. Sign in to Kolik and choose the correct organization.
2. Complete company and employee settings in the **Admin Panel**.
3. Follow the [Leave guide](./admin-panel/izinler/) in order: leave type → policy and entitlement → approval workflow → leave request.

## Go straight to leave management

The [Admin Panel guide](./admin-panel/) links to all its tabs, including company details, users, bank accounts, public holidays, organization structure, timesheets, and organization settings.

- [Create a leave type](./admin-panel/izinler/izin-turu-olusturma) — **Allow Hourly Requests** and **Note Required**.
- [Attach a type to a policy](./admin-panel/izinler/politika-ve-haklar) — annual allowance, accrual, and balance.
- [Leave type rules](./admin-panel/izinler/izin-turu-kurallari) and [calculation settings](./admin-panel/izinler/politika-hesaplama-ayarlari) — detailed policy settings.
- [Create a leave request](./admin-panel/izinler/izin-talebi-olusturma) — employee, time/date, balance, and confirmation.
- [Configure approval workflows](./admin-panel/izinler/onay-akisi) — approver order.

Set [department defaults](./admin-panel/organizasyon/departmanlar) and, if necessary, [license classes](./admin-panel/organizasyon/ehliyet-siniflari) under organization structure.

## Application modules

The [Apps guide](./uygulamalar/) explains how to open and use additional modules. The [App Catalog](./uygulamalar/uygulama-katalogu) covers enabling/disabling apps and catalog administration for super administrators. Fleet, inventory, learning, performance, timesheets, reports, shift calendar, and forms have their own guides.

## Main dashboard sidebar

The [Dashboard and Sidebar guide](./ana-panel/) covers Employees, Leave, Payroll, Meeting Rooms, Support Tickets, Calendar, and AI Assistant with task-oriented steps. Available links depend on your role.

See [My Account](./ana-panel/hesabim/) to edit your profile, add a bank account, select notifications, or change your password.

:::info About application links
Links to the application use the live Kolik address, `https://app.kolik.co/`. You need to sign in with an appropriate organization account to perform these actions.
:::
