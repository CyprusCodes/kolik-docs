# Timesheets

Select **Timesheets** from enabled apps. Its menu has four pages: [Work Entries](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/timesheets), [Payroll Settings](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/timesheets/payroll-settings), [Advances and Deductions](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/timesheets/advances), and [Payroll Periods](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/timesheets/payroll-runs). The last three require company administrator permission.

## Work entries

Select a month and, when needed, an employee to review entries in list or calendar view. Use **Add Entry** to enter the date, working hours, and fields such as overtime or work site. Entries can be edited or deleted from their details; depending on status, approval or rejection actions may also be available. Company administrators can enter **bulk entries** and manage work-site and overtime-type definitions.

## Payroll operations

Manage employee payroll parameters under **Payroll Settings**. Add and follow employee records under **Advances and Deductions**. Track calculations and payroll-run results under **Payroll Periods**. These pages require the enabled app and company administrator role.

Do not confuse work entries in this app with the general definitions under [Admin Panel → Timesheets](../admin-panel/puantaj). The admin tab sets options and rules; this module handles daily work and payroll operations.
