# Performance

Select **Performance** from enabled apps or open its [overview](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/performance). The administrator module menu includes **Overview**, [Cycles](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/performance/cycles), [KPI](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/performance/kpis), [OKR](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/performance/okrs), and [Questionnaires](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/performance/questionnaires). Employees have a personal [My Performance](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/performance/my-performance) page; depending on role and permissions, they may be redirected there from the administrator page.

## Review cycle

The overview shows active, upcoming, and closed cycles. Use **Create Cycle** to set cycle options, dates, participants, and review components step by step. Open an existing cycle to see details and results and, if authorized, edit, copy, or delete it. You can also start and close cycles from **Cycles**. Consider the effect on results before changing a closed cycle.

## KPI and OKR

Under **KPI**, create an indicator, choose its cycle, and track targets and actuals. You can edit or remove an existing indicator. Under **OKR**, create objectives, edit their key results, and record progress updates. The cycle filter narrows results on both pages.

## Questionnaires and personal view

Under **Questionnaires**, manage review question forms and response types. Employees track their reviews and goals from **My Performance**; the current screen may offer report/PDF export. Creation, editing, starting, and deletion depend on user permissions.
