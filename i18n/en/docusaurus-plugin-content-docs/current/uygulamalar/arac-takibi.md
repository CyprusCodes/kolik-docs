# Fleet management

Select **Fleet Management** from enabled apps or open the [vehicle list](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/fleet). This page is designed for company administrators. The module sidebar contains **Vehicles** and [System Settings](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/system-settings).

## Vehicle list

Review vehicles and notices about due or upcoming actions. With **Add Vehicle**, enter basic details such as plate number, make, model, year, fuel type, and status. As needed, also enter vehicle type, mileage, purchase information, color, required driving licence class, owner/assigned employee/department, safety equipment, and photos. You can edit a saved vehicle or open its details from the list.

**Sell Vehicle** is not deletion. It records the buyer, sale date, price, currency, sale type, and notes. Sold vehicles can be viewed in a separate list/filter.

## Vehicle details

Click a vehicle row. The details tabs are **Overview**, **Service**, **Issues**, **Checklist**, **Documents**, **Photos**, and **Activity History**. Add service entries, issue/damage reports, checklist items, documents, and photos. You can edit or remove the relevant entries and review past actions. The vehicle itself may also have a delete option in its details; do not confuse it with selling the vehicle.

## System Settings

Under [System Settings](https://app.kolik.co/dashboard/system-settings), manage tire/battery brands, service locations and types, vehicle makes/types/colors/dealers, vehicle-related people, insurance companies, and legal entities. List screens offer **Add**, row **Edit/Delete** icons, and deletion confirmation. These definitions supply options for vehicle and service forms.
