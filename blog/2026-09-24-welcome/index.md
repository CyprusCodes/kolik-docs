# Introducing Kolik: Your All-in-One HR Platform

Managing HR operations shouldn't be a maze of spreadsheets, emails, and paperwork. Kolik brings leave management, employee management, automatic payment instructions, and support tickets into a single central dashboard — so your team stays one step ahead.

## What is Kolik?

Kolik is a modern HR platform that lets you run all your critical HR processes from one place. Add and manage your employees, define leave types, automate payment instructions, and resolve support requests without juggling multiple tools.

## Key Features

- **Leave Management**: Define multiple leave types and manage the entire approval process from a central command center.

- **Employee Management**: Add, invite, and organize team members in just a few clicks, keeping all employee data secure in one place.

- **Automatic Payment Instructions**: Our automation generates accurate payment instructions for every payment group and sends them to predefined bank contacts automatically.

- **AI Assistant**: Manage leave requests, meeting room reservations, and support tickets through a single intelligent chat.

- **Centralized Support**: Collect every employee request in one place and run your internal support processes professionally.
